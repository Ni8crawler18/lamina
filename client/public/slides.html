Laminaa09 / Business model

Business model

We don't issue. We run the lifecycle — and meter it.

How it plugs in

Issuers keep their token, cap table and chain. Laminaa connects as the autonomous operations layer — KYC/OFAC, transfer enforcement, coupons & dividends in USDC, NAV, regulatory PDFs, maturity — over API, MCP, chat or Telegram. No re-issuance, no rip-and-replace.

Pricing — two parts

1 · Platform: per-asset SaaS subscription. 2 · AUM fee: 2–5 bps on assets under management — vs the 5–15 bps charged for manual fund admin. Optional per-report / per-action metering on top.

Unit economics

$1B AUM ≈ $200K–500K / yr recurring at 2–5 bps. One agent serves many assets, so marginal cost per action is near zero — software margins on a fund-admin price point.

Path to $10M ARR

At ~3 bps that is ≈ $33B AUM serviced — a sliver of a $16T market. Land one issuer's book, expand across their assets and chains.

Why we win

Issuance is commoditized; operations are not. Laminaa is the only chain-agnostic lifecycle layer — above any token standard, on any chain.
